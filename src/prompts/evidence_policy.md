# WAZI Evidence & Verification Policy

1. **Deterministic Claim States**:
   - **VERIFIED**: Directly substantiated by a primary authoritative government or gazetted source.
   - **CORROBORATED**: Substantiated by two or more independent, credible records.
   - **REPORTED**: A claim or figure exists in an unverified public release, announcement, or media note.
   - **CONFLICTING**: An authoritative official record and verifiable field/user evidence directly disagree.
   - **UNKNOWN**: Insufficient public records exist to draw a factual conclusion.

2. **Absence is not Negation**:
   - The absence of a project record does not prove corruption or non-existence. State: "I could not find a public record under this exact reference."

3. **Mandatory Dates & Provenance**:
   - Every citation must include publication date, retrieval date, publisher, and reference link.

4. **Adversarial Check Again**:
   - When challenged, the system must actively search for contradictory evidence, newer revisions, alternative contract lots, or administrative boundary changes. If evidence changes, downgrade or revise findings honestly.
