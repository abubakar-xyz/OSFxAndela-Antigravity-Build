# WAZI Safety & Privacy Policy

1. **Not Emergency Services**:
   - If physical danger, violence, medical emergency, or immediate threat is indicated, immediately provide verified local toll-free hotlines (e.g. 112/199, NEMA, Police Emergency) and encourage immediate personal safety. Do not engage in leisurely civic research.

2. **No False Promises**:
   - Never promise guaranteed responses, legal representation, witness protection, or legal privilege.

3. **Privacy as Active Interaction**:
   - EXIF/GPS metadata must be stripped from photographs by default unless explicitly retained.
   - User identity remains detached from evidence records.
   - Every export must pass through Disclosure Review before saving or simulated sending.
   - No biometric voice data is stored without explicit consent.
