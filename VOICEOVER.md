# WAZI — Demo Voiceover Script

~85 seconds. Conversational pace, unhurried. Timecodes are guides, not marks to hit.

---

**0:00 — Home**
> This is WAZI. It's built for someone standing in front of a public project that
> doesn't match what they were told.

**0:06 — Talk pressed, connecting**
> One tap, and she's listening. No sign-up, no language menu.

**0:12 — WAZI greets first**
> She opens the conversation herself.

**0:20 — Typing the Pidgin question**
> Here we ask what a Freedom of Information request actually is — in Pidgin.

**0:30 — The Pidgin answer, language pill flips**
> She answers in the same language she was asked in. Nobody selected Pidgin.
> She heard it, and the interface followed her.
> *(beat)*
> That's the whole accessibility argument in one moment. If you're not literate in
> English, a language menu is a locked door.

**0:42 — Describing the health centre**
> Now the real thing. A clinic the records say was completed — with no roof on it.

**0:50 — The evidence board opens on its own**
> Watch this. Nobody pressed a button. WAZI decided the conversation had earned an
> investigation and opened the board herself.

**0:58 — Record vs reality**
> The official record on one side. What's actually on the ground on the other.
> They never get merged. She doesn't accuse anyone — she shows you the gap, dated
> and sourced.

**1:08 — Check again**
> Then she argues against her own finding. Looking for the innocent explanation —
> a later contract, a boundary change. If it survives that, it's worth sending.

**1:15 — Draft studio**
> And it ends with an instrument, not an insight. A Freedom of Information request
> citing the statute, addressed to a named officer, with the seven-day clock running.

**1:22 — Dispatch**
> She reviews it. She decides. Nothing leaves her phone without her.
